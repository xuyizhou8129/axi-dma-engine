To specify a normal (no-error) test case, use:
make sim SCENARIO=scenarios/TESTNAME.csv
For example, to test max flight:
make sim SCENARIO=scenarios/max_inflight.csv

To specify an intended error-handling test case, use:
make sim-expected-error SCENARIO=scenarios/error_chain_good_bad_good.csv

Running sim-all will:
Run all test cases and record what was passed and 
what was not in out/sim-all-report.txt

Since all error scenarios have a flag in front, make sim-all will recognize it,
 and call sim-expected-error for those cases automatically
